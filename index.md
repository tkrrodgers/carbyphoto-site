CarByPhoto identifies a car from a photo. When the photo isn't enough, Joe, the app's car expert, asks a quick
question (like "What logo is on the grille?"). Then he shows the car's specs, fuel economy, recalls and safety
ratings, and answers your questions about it.

- [Privacy policy](privacy-policy.html)
- Support: TestFlight testers can write to us from TestFlight → CarByPhoto → Send Beta Feedback.

CarByPhoto is made by Prosperity Simplified. It has no account, no ads and no tracking.
